## Pricing and Inventory Decision

No Noah selling price has been approved. The local NDS-034 record contains a TZS 45,000 retailer reference solely to document the market evidence; its card explicitly says the Noah price is unapproved. Retailer prices cannot substitute for Noah's cost basis. To calculate a reasonable price, provide the product acquisition cost and estimated inbound/outbound delivery, payment fees, packaging, and desired margin. For each item Noah intends to carry, confirm stock and warranty separately. NDS-034 has stock 0 and is not purchasable until those details are approved.

Retailer prices/availability can change; verify again before using this research to make a buying or selling decision. Product images on retailer pages have not been treated as licensed Noah assets and should not be hotlinked.
